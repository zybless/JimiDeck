# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

JimiDeck welcomes respectful participation regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint. Be direct about technical disagreements without making them personal. Give useful context, respect privacy, and assume that contributors may have different constraints or communication styles.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/zybless). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## 中文说明

JimiDeck 欢迎不同经验、身份、背景与观点的参与者。技术意见可以直接表达，但不应针对个人。请提供有用的上下文、尊重隐私，并理解贡献者可能拥有不同的条件和沟通方式。

项目空间不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视性言论、性化关注、蓄意恐吓或公开他人私人信息。维护者可以编辑或移除违反准则的内容，并临时或永久限制相关人员参与。行为问题请通过[维护者 GitHub 主页](https://github.com/zybless)列出的联系方式私下报告；项目会在实际可行的范围内保密处理。

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
