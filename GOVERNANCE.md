# Project Governance

## English

JimiDeck uses a maintainer-led model. Maintainers set project direction, review changes, manage releases, and apply the Code of Conduct.

Technical decisions favor local data ownership, predictable recovery, platform-native behavior, and a small trusted surface. Significant behavior changes should be proposed in an issue before implementation. Maintainers may decline work that expands the product beyond its profile-management scope or cannot be maintained safely.

Roles are earned through sustained, constructive contributions. A maintainer may invite a contributor to take responsibility for an area after a record of reliable reviews or changes. Project roles do not imply employment or a service-level commitment.

## 中文

JimiDeck 采用维护者主导的治理方式。维护者负责确定项目方向、审查变更、管理发布并执行行为准则。

技术决策优先考虑本地数据所有权、可预测的恢复机制、平台原生行为和较小的可信范围。明显改变产品行为的方案应先通过 Issue 讨论。对于超出配置管理范围或无法安全维护的功能，维护者可以不予接受。

项目角色来自持续、建设性的贡献。贡献者在某一领域长期提交可靠变更或审查后，维护者可以邀请其共同负责该领域。项目角色不代表雇佣关系，也不包含服务等级承诺。
